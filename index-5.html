How It Started 🕰️

February 2022 — Roblox.

One night, Asif was playing YouTuber Tycoon when he noticed Mari being bullied. He helped her, and the two of them started playing together. A friendship began.

A few days later, Asif came back from school. Mari asked if he had been the person playing with her. He said no — he had been at school. She told him that someone had said they liked her, and she had replied, “me too.”

Asif realized he couldn't keep it hidden. He confessed his feelings.

And somehow, from one Roblox night and one act of kindness, Asifuna and Mari became boyfriend and girlfriend — and best friends too.

🌍 Long distance, but never far in the heart. 🍒🥝
